Your College Journey Starts with Clarity

Student Compass helps first-year students understand their degree, semester, skills, and career path — all in one place.

Why Student Compass?

Many students enter college without knowing how their degree works, what subjects they will study, how internal assessments, semester exams, credits, CGPA, projects, internships, and placements fit together. This confusion makes it hard to plan their college journey and career path from the beginning. Student Compass solves this by giving students a clear roadmap for their academics, skills, and future opportunities.

What Student Compass Gives You

Semester Guidance

Understand subjects, semester flow, credits, and internal assessments clearly.

Skill Roadmap

Know which technical and soft skills you should learn apart from college subjects.

Career Direction

Explore job opportunities, internships, higher studies, and career options for your degree.
